Brutalist Badge is a direct styled-only label with no Base counterpart.

## Contract

- Square geometry, `2px` ink border, and a small hard shadow in every tone.
- Public `tone` is `accent`, `info`, or `danger`; each tone pairs its flat fill with the intended foreground.
- `outline` is structural grammar, not a variant. Badge has no pill radius, gradient, glass, or glow.
- Passive, roleless, and non-focusable: Badge owns no status announcement, activation, pressed, selected, command, or event channel.

Use Badge for passive labels such as roles or environments. Dynamic status belongs to a live-region owner; navigation belongs to Link; activation belongs to Button; pressed state belongs to Toggle.
