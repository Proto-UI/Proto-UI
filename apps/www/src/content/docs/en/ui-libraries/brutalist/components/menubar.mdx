## Usage

### Anatomy

Menubar is composed of four parts: `Root`, `Trigger`, `Content`, and `Item`.

- `Root`: Manages the active panel value and coordinates keyboard navigation across the bar.
- `Trigger`: Opens or switches to the menu panel matching its `value`.
- `Content`: Renders the command panel paired with a `Trigger` by `value`.
- `Item`: Represents a selectable command inside a panel.

### Keyboard & Focus

- The bar participates as a single roving `Tab` stop; `ArrowLeft` and `ArrowRight` move between triggers.
- `ArrowDown` and `ArrowUp` open a panel and move vertically through items, with typeahead character matching.
- Pressing `Escape` dismisses the open panel and returns focus to its matching `Trigger`. Pressing outside or selecting an item also dismisses the panel unless `closeOnSelect` is set to `false` on the `Item`.

### Controlled vs. Uncontrolled

- Use `defaultValue` on `Root` for uncontrolled state.
- Pass `value` on `Root` for controlled mode; updates emit change requests so your state controls which panel is open.

Other part props and defaults:

- **Trigger**: `value` defaults to `''`; `disabled` defaults to `false`.
- **Content**: `value` and `a11yLabel` default to `''`; `sideOffset` defaults to `4`.
- **Item**: `value` and `textValue` default to `''`; `disabled` defaults to `false`; `closeOnSelect` defaults to `true`.
