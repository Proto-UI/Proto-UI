Switch toggles a setting between on and off states. Its Brutalist presentation pairs a 2px black-bordered capsule track with a white circular thumb and no drop shadow for crisp, mechanical feedback.

## Installation

Included in `@proto.ui/prototypes-brutalist@0.2.0`. Add it via the CLI target `brutalist-switch`, or import directly from `@proto.ui/prototypes-brutalist/switch`.

## Usage

### Visual & Motion Details

- **Geometry & colors**: At a 16px root font size, the track is `24×48px` and the white circular thumb is `16px`. The track switches from neutral (off) to main blue (on) while the thumb travels `1.25rem` (20px with symmetric 6px outer insets).
- **State feedback**: The `150ms` thumb transition becomes instantaneous under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Pressing does not shift the track or add shadow; keyboard focus displays an offset ring; `disabled` preserves geometry and blocks activation.
- **Labelling & layout**: Place the visible caption inside `Root` to supply the accessible name and click target (DM Sans typography with host fallback). Physical left/right thumb coordinates keep geometry bounded even inside RTL parents.

### Interaction Notes

- Supports click and keyboard activation, as well as controlled mode via `checked`.
- Pointer dragging is not enabled yet: `draggable` defaults to `false`, and passing `true` falls back to `false` while click and keyboard toggling continue to work normally.
