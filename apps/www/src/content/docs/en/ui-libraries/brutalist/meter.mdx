Brutalist Meter displays a scalar measurement within a known range—such as storage usage, quota, or score—with threshold classification based on `low`, `high`, and `optimum`.

## Installation

Currently available from workspace source; not yet included in the 0.2.0 stable release.

- **Package exports**: import `meterRoot`, `meterLabel`, `meterTrack`, `meterIndicator`, and `meterValue` from `@proto.ui/prototypes-brutalist/meter`

## Usage

### Parts

- **Root**: Manages `value`, finite bounds `min` and `max`, and optional `low`, `high`, and `optimum` thresholds. Values clamp to `[min, max]`, and equal bounds produce `0` percentage.
- **Label**: Provides the accessible label for the meter.
- **Track / Indicator**: Render the bar container and filled indicator proportional to the computed percentage.
- **Value**: Displays `valueText` when set, otherwise the clamped numeric value.

### Thresholds and accessibility

- Setting `low`, `high`, and `optimum` classifies the current reading as `'optimal'`, `'suboptimal'`, or `'critical'` without modifying the underlying value.
- Meter is a passive readout with no keyboard tab stop; `Root` and its parts stay synchronized and expose `value`, `percentage`, `indeterminate`, and `status`.
