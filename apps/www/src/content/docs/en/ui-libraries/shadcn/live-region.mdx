## Usage

Currently available from workspace source; not yet included in the 0.2.0 stable release.

`Live Region` consists of a single `Root` part. Your children form the announcement payload — update the rendered text to deliver a new announcement:

- `politeness="polite"` (default): Applies `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` for non-interruptive updates.
- `politeness="assertive"`: Applies `role="alert"` and `aria-live="assertive"` for urgent messages.
- `atomic={true}` (default): Sets `aria-atomic="true"` so the entire region is announced together when content changes.

`Live Region` adds no focus stops, events, or exposes, and pairs cleanly with [Async Region](../async-region/) or form error feedback.
