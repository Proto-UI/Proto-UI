The region derives `role`, `aria-live` and `aria-atomic` from two props: `politeness: 'polite'` (default) projects `role=status` with `aria-live=polite`, and `politeness: 'assertive'` projects `role=alert` with `aria-live=assertive`; `atomic` (default `true`) projects `aria-atomic`. Authored child content is the announcement payload — mutating it is how updates are delivered. The region itself declares no focus, event, command, selection, or accessible-name override channel, and the exposes surface is empty; there is no implicit queue or notification transport.
