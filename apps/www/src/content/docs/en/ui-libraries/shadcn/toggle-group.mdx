value/defaultValue are arrays. multiple=false keeps at most one value; clicking it again clears selection. Controlled valueChange requests never mutate the owner. Disabled, readOnly and duplicate-valued items do not commit. Arrow navigation changes focus without selecting.

Parts: Root, Item.
