## Usage

```tsx
import {
  LiquidGlassDrawerRoot,
  LiquidGlassDrawerTrigger,
  LiquidGlassDrawerMask,
  LiquidGlassDrawerContent,
  LiquidGlassDrawerHandle,
  LiquidGlassDrawerHeader,
  LiquidGlassDrawerTitle,
  LiquidGlassDrawerDescription,
  LiquidGlassDrawerFooter,
  LiquidGlassDrawerClose,
} from '../proto-ui/components/react';
```

```tsx
<LiquidGlassDrawerRoot>
  <LiquidGlassDrawerTrigger variant="prominent">Open Drawer</LiquidGlassDrawerTrigger>
  <LiquidGlassDrawerMask />
  <LiquidGlassDrawerContent side="bottom">
    <LiquidGlassDrawerHandle />
    <LiquidGlassDrawerHeader>
      <LiquidGlassDrawerTitle>Spatial Audio</LiquidGlassDrawerTitle>
      <LiquidGlassDrawerDescription>
        Adaptive head tracking and studio clarity for nearby devices.
      </LiquidGlassDrawerDescription>
    </LiquidGlassDrawerHeader>
    <div className="flex-1 overflow-y-auto px-4">{/* Content */}</div>
    <LiquidGlassDrawerFooter>
      <LiquidGlassDrawerClose variant="prominent">Done</LiquidGlassDrawerClose>
    </LiquidGlassDrawerFooter>
  </LiquidGlassDrawerContent>
</LiquidGlassDrawerRoot>
```

### Composition & material hierarchy

```text
DrawerRoot
├── DrawerTrigger
├── DrawerMask
└── DrawerContent
    ├── DrawerHandle
    ├── DrawerHeader
    │   ├── DrawerTitle
    │   └── DrawerDescription
    └── DrawerFooter
        └── DrawerClose
```

- **No glass-on-glass stacking**: `Content` and page-level `Trigger` declare v2 Liquid Glass material, while interior controls (`Close`, `Header`, `Footer`) remain clean non-glass surfaces inside the sheet.
- **Prominence and fallbacks**: Use `variant="regular" | "prominent"` on `Trigger` and `Close` to separate primary from secondary actions. Pass `material="opaque"` on `Content` or `Trigger` to opt into the solid fallback, which also activates automatically under reduced transparency or unsupported hosts.
- **Motion and snapping**: `Content` animates smoothly over 320ms enter / 240ms leave (respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`) over a soft `bg-black/30` scrim. Pass `(0, 1]` fractions in `snapPoints` on `Content` for multi-stop dragging and arrow-key resizing.

## API

### Root

Pass `open` for controlled mode and subscribe to `openChange`. Exposes `openDrawer()`, `close()`, and `toggle()`.

### Content

Exposes `open`, `snapPoint`, `dragging`, `dragProgress`, `offsetPercentage`, `extentValid`, `requestSnapPoint()`, and `snapPointChange`.
