Liquid Glass Autocomplete pairs a legible editing input with floating concentric Liquid Glass suggestion chrome, supporting `regular` and `prominent` trigger hierarchy, `material="opaque"` fallbacks, live filtering, and free-text submission.

## Installation

Currently available from workspace source (`@proto.ui/prototypes-liquid-glass/autocomplete`); not yet included in the 0.2.0 stable release.

## Usage

```html
<proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-root a11y-label="Framework">
  <proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-input
    placeholder="Search frameworks…"
  ></proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-input>
  <proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-trigger variant="regular" material="auto">
    Options
  </proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-trigger>
  <proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-content material="auto">
    <proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-item value="next" text-value="Next.js" keywords="react ssr">
      Next.js
    </proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-item>
    <proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-item
      value="astro"
      text-value="Astro"
      keywords="islands static"
    >
      Astro
    </proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-item>
    <proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-empty
      >No matching frameworks.</proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-empty
    >
  </proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-content>
</proto-ui-liquid-glass-autocomplete-root>
```

### Material layering and behavior

- **Layering and concentric radii**: `Input` stays an opaque editing surface for text legibility while `Trigger` and `Content` declare v2 `liquid-glass` material without stacking glass on glass. `Content` (`rounded-2xl` + `p-1`) and `Item` (`rounded-xl`) maintain concentric corner geometry.
- **Prominence and fallbacks**: `Trigger` supports `variant="regular" | "prominent"`, and both `Trigger` and `Content` support `material="auto" | "opaque"`. Disabled states withdraw glass material and keep readable semantic fallback tokens.
- **Filtering and keyboard**: Built-in filtering folds case and diacritics across `textValue` and `keywords`. Pressing `Enter` without an active match commits custom free text, and IME composition never prematurely commits an item.
