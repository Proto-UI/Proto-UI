## Installation

Available starting in `0.3.0-rc.1` and later releases. Import `meterRoot`, `meterLabel`, `meterTrack`, `meterIndicator`, and `meterValue` from `@proto.ui/prototypes-base/meter`.

## Usage

Compose `Root`, `Label`, `Track`, `Indicator`, and `Value`:

- **Composition roles**: `Root` manages the numeric range; `Label` names the meter, `Track` contains `Indicator`, and `Value` displays `valueText` or the formatted numeric value.
- **Clamping and thresholds**: `value` clamps to ordered finite `[min, max]` bounds (an equal `min` and `max` yields `0` percentage). Optional `low`, `high`, and `optimum` classify `status` as `optimal`, `suboptimal`, or `critical` without altering the underlying number.
- **Passive readout**: Meter is display-only with no keyboard tab stop or editing events. Updating props synchronizes every part. `Root` and its parts expose `value`, `percentage`, `indeterminate`, and `status`.
