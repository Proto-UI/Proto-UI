## Installation

Currently available from workspace source; not yet included in the 0.2.0 stable release. Import `progressRoot`, `progressLabel`, `progressTrack`, `progressIndicator`, and `progressValue` from `@proto.ui/prototypes-base/progress`.

## Usage

Compose `Root`, `Label`, `Track`, `Indicator`, and `Value`:

- **Composition roles**: `Root` manages the numeric range; `Label` names the progress bar, `Track` holds `Indicator`, and `Value` displays `valueText` or the formatted number.
- **Determinate and indeterminate modes**: `value` clamps to ordered finite `[min, max]` bounds (equal bounds yield `0` percentage). Omitting `value` or setting `indeterminate={true}` marks progress as unknown and withholds an accessible numeric value.
- **Passive readout**: Progress is display-only with no keyboard tab stop or editing events. Updating props synchronizes every part. `Root` and its parts expose `value`, `percentage`, `indeterminate`, and `status`.
