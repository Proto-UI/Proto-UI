# 0.3 release delivery list

Production deadline: **2026-11-04 23:59 Asia/Shanghai**. Feature integration target: **2026-11-08 23:59**. Acceptance/repair stage: November 9–22; RC: November 23–27; final decision: November 28–30. See [the governing plan](release-0.3-plan.md).

This is a source inventory, **not a claim that these capabilities have passed acceptance**. The frozen [source ledger](release-0.3-source-ledger.json) binds package surfaces, CLI entries, 203 original Finf comment checkboxes, 68 named delivery criteria, and the 187-open-Issue snapshot to their sources. It captured 1,233 Issue checkbox conditions; the additive [source corrections](release-0.3-source-corrections.json) restore five omitted conditions, giving the current effective denominator of **1,238**. Historical checks remain historical; each current development and acceptance result needs its own exact source and evidence. Mixed Issue conditions retain `criterion-split` until their concrete family and prerequisite role are reconciled; the snapshot does not authorize title-only closure or deferral. Counts of export roots or aliases are not counts of independent Prototypes.

Repository file fingerprints in the ledger bind to its explicit `sourceRevision`, not to later worktree bytes. In particular, the delivery checklist fingerprint preserves the complete pre-preface source at that revision; the additive release-stage preface does not replace that historical source or certify acceptance.

## Additive evidence checkpoints

The [evidence checkpoints](release-0.3-evidence-checkpoints.json) extend this same source-bound ledger with timestamped child evidence for #1073 and #1191. They bind the unchanged source snapshot by Git blob and SHA-256, retain original Issue/criterion keys, and separate verified contribution owners from unconfirmed acceptance owners. They are not a second inventory, do not override original criteria, and do not close either Issue. Development integration, Draft publication, source-only tests, skipped CI and native/packed acceptance remain distinct. New inventory versions must preserve this historical snapshot rather than silently rebinding it.

Validate the snapshot/companion and its negative controls with `node --test scripts/ci/test/release-source-ledger-checkpoints.test.mjs` on Node.js 24.

## Source corrections

The [source corrections](release-0.3-source-corrections.json) record bounded acquisition repairs against the same frozen snapshot. The first correction restores Issue #649's five original acceptance conditions (source lines 51–55), which the initial acquisition omitted from its CRLF source body. The frozen snapshot keeps that omission as captured. `loadEffectiveLedger` in `scripts/ci/release-source-ledger.mjs` supplies current Issue criterion identities and denominators, including checkpoint admission. It verifies the frozen bytes, Git blob and revision, hashes the retained complete Issue body, and checks each restored line, section and original checkbox state. LF and CRLF acquisition produce identical criteria. A correction cannot rewrite, duplicate, reclassify or complete a condition; current evidence remains in the separate checkpoint companion.
