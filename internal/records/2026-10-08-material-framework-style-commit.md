## Cause and repair

The shared sink requests final style, then immediately validates the host's actual tokens before checking geometry and admitting paint. React's EffectsPort only queued a state update; Vue/Vue2 similarly deferred DOM style through framework rendering. The sink correctly rejected their still-old DOM style and withdrew paint. A later DOM mutation plus geometry-observer RAF restored it. No independent style acknowledgement ticket existed to lose.

For an Adapter configured with `createVisualSink`, its current physical view now commits its own `data-pui-style` attribute synchronously before returning from the EffectsPort. Its framework VNode entirely omits this attribute, so a delayed or unrelated render cannot overwrite the newer projection. Vue2 specifically requires omitting the key: retaining an undefined VNode attribute causes later `updateAttrs` removal. The provider choice is captured once when defining the adapted component. A factory returning null keeps the same synchronous ordinary-style path; adapters without a configured factory retain their existing declarative path.

Framework token state/reveal revision bookkeeping remains and happens before the observable DOM write. Both before and after that bookkeeping, the writer checks its retired flag and captured physical root. Cleanup first retires the writer and removes only its still-owned last attribute value, preserving an author's replacement. EffectsPorts drain a newer queued style revision after synchronous reentry, rather than dropping a flush issued by a custom-element attribute callback. Actual custom-element callbacks exercise both replacement and synchronous view disposal.

The shared sink, its pending guard, source/carrier inspection, shader, and original browser assertions are unchanged. After style is current, its existing complete source/preferences/geometry/paint-lease admission determines whether old pixels remain compatible. The repair neither creates a special exemption for pending style nor holds arbitrary old images across a safety veto.

Authority remains draft `C-VISUAL-TRANSACTION-0001` snapshot, identity, stale-completion and fallback criteria; `HC-FEEDBACK-VISUAL-SINK-0001` lifetime/evidence criteria; and `C-FEEDBACK-MATERIAL-0001` ownership, safety and quality criteria. This repair does not promote those entities or claim new stable, native or cross-engine support.
