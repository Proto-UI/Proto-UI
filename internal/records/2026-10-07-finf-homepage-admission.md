# Finf homepage exhibition and admission

The maintainer requires three additional homepage outcomes within the existing Finf scope:

- Every newly introduced prototype needs a catalog-driven interactive homepage exhibition and state set built from actual atoms, with normal source-bound interaction, accessibility and visual acceptance.
- Bootstrap 2.3.2 Classic and Liquid Glass may enter the homepage family selector only after complete Base identity/atomic parity and mature applicable implementation and evidence. Source-stage entries remain ineligible.
