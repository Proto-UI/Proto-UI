## Meaning retained by each keep decision

| Figure | Reason and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| information-flow-model | Keep the canonical chapter 2 comparison and the original State/Lifecycle/Meta content. Anatomy does not rename Meta; these internal dimensions are not additional channels. Draft legacy articles are retained references, not production routes. |
| component-anatomy | Keep the Root/Thumb ownership comparison and the independent Select parts. The fixed Caret is a boundary example, not a new Base part or a prescribed Host tree. |
| component-boundary | Keep the author layout: independent Event/Props/Expose/Context requires a Component; Feedback-only structure may remain attached. Preserve the distinction between may and must. |
| conditional-consistency | Keep the obligations baseline and aligned condition-to-comparison rows. The rows are not automatic tiers and do not claim complete cross-Host evidence. |
| evolution-feedback | Keep the three concurrent work lines, evidence, four alternative diagnoses and explicit revise/test-again loop. The mobile layout preserves the same meaning; its readability requires its own evidence. |
| information-channels | Keep the five directed relations between User, App Maker and Component. Context allows both directions without specifying callback scheduling; the bilingual article supplies the explanation. |
| lifecycle | Keep the author timeline separating setup, repeatable mount epochs and terminal disposal. A repeated mount is not a new instance and detach is not terminal disposal. |
| switch-activation | Keep the landed uncontrolled, enabled Switch example: Root owns checked; Expose reaches App Maker; Context reaches Thumb; both request their own Feedback. Arrow placement does not promise synchronous callbacks. |
| translation-responsibility | Keep the runtime Adapter responsibility map, required host facts/operations and attribution. Its illustrative Flutter/Qt targets do not promise equal delivery or describe the thin compiler path. |
