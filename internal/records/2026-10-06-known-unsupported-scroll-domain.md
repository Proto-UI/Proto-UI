Date: 2026-10-06 UTC. This layer follows the separate rounded-overflow domain guard. Tracking: [#853](https://github.com/Proto-UI/Proto-UI/issues/853). ## Admission is deliberately narrow

Only the current `scroll-area` case's initial `rest` frame can enter `known-unsupported`, before any achieved target. The primary must be the exact `brutalist-scroll-area-viewport`, with intersecting bounds, unsupported classification and exactly the rounded-overflow limitation. Its one clip must be the current owner/generation's `brutalist-scroll-area-root`, hidden overflow on both axes, fixed-px geometry with a positive safe central rectangle and primary-box overlap with that rectangle. Interior overlap is a geometry fact, not a painted/numeric acceptance claim.

Both projection and anatomy observations must pass. Before entering the terminal outcome, the case must also repeat the original end-of-case served/local source provenance verification. A source-drift error follows the ordinary failed-case journal path and cannot become a known-unsupported success. All three fingerprints, before/after ownership, measurement leases, PNG/fact receipts and original state predicate must agree. The facts must include exactly one Root and Viewport; every physical surface must retain visible bounds and have either the supported domain or that same owned rounded-clip limitation. A truly hidden surface, unknown clipping/paint domain, foreign owner, replacement, changed epoch, missing anatomy, missing image/facts or fingerprint mismatch still fails normally.

The primary-paint decision moves after the unchanged after-fingerprint/lease/anatomy checks so even an unsupported raw frame has complete pairing evidence. The case then terminates without later input journeys. No target is appended to `achievedTargets`; original planned and missing targets remain. The raw frame is `known-unsupported`, not `matched`, hidden, exempt, achieved or numerically acceptable. The journal preserves its image, facts, source identity and limitation.

## Summary and exit semantics

Reports separately derive expected cases, observed cases, known-unsupported cases, paired-but-unmeasured raw frames, their unexecuted targets, and genuine unresolved cases. The final console lists known-unsupported runtime/theme coordinates and #853. Known-unsupported is a successfully recorded limitation, not a successful measurement; the disposition explicitly says so. Any real failed/pending/incomplete case, fatal error or cleanup failure still makes the run fail. No family disappears from the discovered case matrix.

The expected affected domain is the current ScrollArea recipe across its page-declared runtimes/themes. Predicted counts are not observations. Exact counts and any additional unsupported domain must come from the new native run; unexpected domains cannot use this exception. Existing Scroll semantics remain separate from the stopped contrast journeys.

## Verification boundary

Controlled policy tests reject alternative families, later states, prior achieved targets, hidden primary/parts, extra domains, relative radii, no safe interior, extra clips, owner/epoch changes, missing anatomy, failed predicates, mismatched fingerprints and missing raw receipts. The actual `capture` and actual case-catch code are executed with controlled transport: known-domain raw/facts remain paired, no achieved target is added, and hidden/identity/source-drift faults stay failed/unresolved. Journal replay proves that raw unknown frames do not increment matched frames or achieved targets and retain planned targets.

These are controlled source/transport checks, not native paint or interaction evidence. Native calibration, pixel inspection and final case accounting must bind to the published combined tree.
