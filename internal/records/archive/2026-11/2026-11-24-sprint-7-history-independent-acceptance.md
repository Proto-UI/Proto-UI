## Corrected remaining-work routing

The old producer's keyword search treated any line containing a follow-up/remaining/tracking term and an open tracked Issue reference as a positive execution transfer. That is insufficient when the sentence explicitly excludes the target, describes an expired handoff, lists an aggregate parent, or discusses several independent topics.

The independently read references now have these dispositions:

| --- | --: | --- |

| `historical-authorization` | 46 | Preserve the old permission/claim/queue record; do not renew authority |
| `excluded` | 15 | Preserve the explicit boundary; do not assign excluded work to that target |
| **Total** | **496** | No source occurrence is lost |

The old ledger turned the three open tracked references into positive tasks. All three are now retained as exclusions. 3. **[#420](https://github.com/Proto-UI/Proto-UI/issues/420), mixed documentation/menu paragraph.** Only the Apple Liquid menu-state subcondition belongs to #1149. Documentation double-focus and FAQ work retain their own owners. The corrected remaining condition names that limited Liquid scope and keeps the entire original paragraph in its source reference.

Other corrections preserve portfolio headings as headings, landed evidence links as evidence, source-dependency references as dependencies, and historical claims as historical. Explicit duplicate transfers and genuine remaining acceptance routes remain intact. For example, #1676 still points to #1156 for Finf enrollment and affected-consumer closeout; it does not recreate the completed Select bool implementation task.
